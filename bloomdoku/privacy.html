Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Bloomdoku collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it is suitable for players of all ages, including children.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (garden, statistics, settings and saved games) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

When you use Scan a Puzzle, the photo is processed entirely on your device by Apple's Vision framework to read the numbers. Images are never uploaded, stored or shared. When you choose a photo from your library, Bloomdoku can only see the single photo you pick.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. A parental gate (a question for grown-ups) appears before any purchase.

Children

Bloomdoku does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
