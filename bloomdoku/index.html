Features

So much more than a grid.

🌸

A garden that grows

Every solve plants a bloom. Harder and perfect solves grow rare and legendary ones, and unlock new landmarks.

🐣

Kids play with Ninelings

4×4 and 6×6 grids with nine creature friends instead of numbers. Logic before digits!

📸

Scan any Sudoku

Snap a puzzle from a book, newspaper or screen. It's read on your device in seconds.

💡

Hints that teach

Where to look, why it works, then the answer. Learn real techniques as you play.

🎵

A musical grid

Every number plays a note. Finish a row or box and hear a happy little tune.

⚔️

Daily Bloom & Duels

A fresh puzzle every day with streaks, plus face-to-face family races on one device.
